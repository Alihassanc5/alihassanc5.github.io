Healthcare AI · built at Cogent Labs

Dr C for Chughtai Lab

Built as part of my role at Cogent Labs, for Chughtai Lab, a well-known diagnostic lab in Pakistan.

Dr C is the AI health assistant inside the Chughtai Lab mobile app. Patients chat or talk to it to check test prices, book home sampling and understand their lab reports.

  • Answers test price, report time and home sampling questions from the lab's catalogue
  • Books home sampling in the chat using the patient's saved addresses
  • Summarises lab reports in plain language and highlights results outside normal range
  • Voice calls with the assistant through OpenAI's Realtime API
